# Fneb as a tabletop-dm world: decisions

Settled 2026-10-24, from Matt's notes in `~/Documents/writing/fneb/`.
This file records decisions only. The world is drafted here in
`worlds/fneb/`, and ships once it moves to
`skills/tabletop-dm/worlds/fneb/`.

## Settled

1. **One map.** The capital on the Ghost Sea, the lowlands, the hill
   country, the kingdom border, the barrens and the cursed desert, the
   stone valley, and the Fneb mountains. The wraith's stronghold (now
   the mountain shrine) sits over the frozen fiend: one Old One
   fragment, the giant eye in the gem.

2. **Three plagues, three causes.** The black-fingernail plague that
   comes on the spring ships is natural. Ashmouth is a night druid's
   revenge. The black spores leak from Old One fragments.

3. **The witch.** A church purge-priest burned her grove. Her lost son
   was taken and raised by the church, and he is now the new
   druid-hating priest.

4. **One church, split into factions.** The lantern faith (scholars and
   musicians) against a hardline purging faction. The corrupt cleric of
   "Reclaim the Relic" and the new priest of the farm story are the same
   man.

5. **The demon cabal** are surviving Young Ones who never withered, and
   who want their masters back.

6. **Tone:** grim, with comic relief (the canary, the turnips, odd inns
   and odd people).

7. **The "everybody dies" one-shot is left out.** The engine's normal
   death rules apply.

8. **Druids become a player class.** This means adding the SRD druid
   class to the engine's data (separate work, with tests).

9. **Missing monsters** (wraith, giant scorpion, roper) are custom stat
   blocks in the seeds, not new rules data.

10. **House rules kept:** the six four-hour watches go in the bible, the
    farm door gets farming as downtime, and the oasis door keeps the
    desert navigation.

11. **The world is named Fneb** (world id `fneb`).

12. **Reddit ideas kept and credited:** the old-man shakedown hook
    (Boojamon) and the plague-spitting bandit (MelancholyOcelot).

13. **The magical advice canary is in, with a secret agenda:** it is one
    of many avatars playing a centuries-long game, and that game is a
    metaplot thread.

14. **`modern-druids.txt` is left out.**

15. **Order of work:** draft `bible.md` first; the druid class follows
    as a separate, tested change.

16. **Names:** the capital is Haldmouth, its river is the Hald, the
    empty city of the night druid legend is Vessary, and the kingdom
    across the desert stays the Eastern Kingdom.
