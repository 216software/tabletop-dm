# Fneb: credits

Fneb was created by W. Matthew Wilson, from his campaign "Who Let the
Unholy Out?", his Fneb notes and his druid lore, and was shaped into a
tabletop-dm world with Claude.

Two ideas in the oasis door came from readers on Reddit's r/rpg, in
reply to a draft of "The Oasis in the Desert":

- **Boojamon**, for the opening hook in the market.

- **MelancholyOcelot**, for the plague-carrying highwayman.
