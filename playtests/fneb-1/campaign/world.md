# World

## People

- **Corra Vane**: the hero. A rogue from the Haldmouth docks, taken in by Granny Moss.
- **Granny Moss**: small, sharp, very old. Her farm is an hour's walk up the valley, at the edge of the elf woods. Took Corra in. Brother Severin wants her to call on him at the chapel.
- **Tam Moss**: Granny's grandson, a fighter. Big, gentle, stubborn. Travels with Corra. A Scythe soldier says he has seen Tam going into the elf woods, twice.
- **Ottilie Pellow**: the tax collector's daughter and clerk, a wizard. Thinks her father's bad dreams are his conscience. Ink on her cuffs. Hid Corra and Tam in the counting house and owes Corra a favour (nothing hanging). Corra keeps a secret of hers.
- **Old Bryn**: hedge-priest of the Green Mother.
- **Brother Severin**: the valley's new Lantern Church priest. Young, tired eyes, ink on two fingers, warm voice. Doubled the rent, banned dealing with elves, ordered the dead buried. Saw through Corra's false name ("Wenna"; his nurse was a Wenna).
- **Captain Rusk**: commands the Scythe Company. Neat orange coat, polite, tidy, bored. Has a clerk who reads out orders like onion prices.
- **Master Pellow**: the tax collector. Soft, grey, shiny-elbowed coat. Took the church's sixth for nineteen years; now takes a third. Dreams every night of a smiling stranger who thanks him for every coin.
- **Constable Moe**: ancient, leans on a huge war hammer, says he killed a one-eyed troll with it.
- **The auctioneer**: fast talking.
- **Willow**: Tam's name for the elf girl he meets at the ribbon box at dusk on bright moon nights. Pale hair. She brings him a leaf-wrapped cake.
- **The Scythe sergeant**: grey-haired, unhurried, did not scatter when the mule charged. Wants a tall man and a girl.
- **Three soldiers and two war dogs**: laying a rope ring and open mead jugs in Granny's lower field.

## Places

- **Broadscale**: the Fneb valley's market town, with the great scales where farmers pay rent in crops. A north lane runs out of the square past an alley behind the pie stalls.
- **Granny Moss's farm**: up the valley, at the edge of the elf woods.
- **The chapel**: where Severin receives callers.
- **Pellow's counting house**: narrow stone building behind the pie stalls, with a barrel yard and a coal hatch into the grain lane.
- **Granny's lower field**: by the edge of the elf woods, with ribbons on sticks in a row. The ribbons are a language read at the elf end. A black ribbon tied three knots, left over right, on the third stick says: not tonight, the field is shut.

## Factions

- **The Lantern Church**: owns the valley's land; rent is paid in crops. Rent now doubled.
- **The Scythe Company**: mercenaries in orange under a black scythe banner, about twenty in Broadscale, hired by the church. Led by Captain Rusk.
