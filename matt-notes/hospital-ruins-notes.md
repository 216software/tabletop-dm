# Hospital Ruins Notes

## Analog Memory Transfer lab

The lab is empty but in near pristine condition and is full of equipment
that would be very valuable to black market buyers.  Also, regular
corporate buyers would be interested as well, but going through the
regular methods means way less payouts.

## Secret research data

The moon AIs are mostly interested in research done at the hospital
before the collapse.  The moon AIs want any data that can be collected.

The research was focused on using engineered viruses to undo the effects
of genetic mutations.  Scientists collected mutated insects (some have
become monstrously large and are now roving the hospital) and were
experimenting with new viruses that would reverse their mutations.
